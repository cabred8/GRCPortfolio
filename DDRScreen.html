Dance Dance Revolution Screen Border Recreation

Made in February of 2026, the piece is a recreation of a Dance Dance Revolution screen border. This was made out of necessity, as existing replicas were too low quality for my needs. The piece was made by image tracing graphics on Adobe Illustrator and adding text, which required counting rows of text for accuracy. This was used to frame "Can I Call You a Dancin' Master?" which was displayed at the Donna Beam Art Gallery as a part of the BA Call for Entry Exhibition.
