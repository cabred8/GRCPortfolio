Diego Capybara (Diego Cabrera Quintanilla) is a student based in Las Vegas, Nevada.

He is working towards a degree in Graphic Design at UNLV, and with it, a different perspective at tackling design issues. Diego leads projects with a tight schedule and attention to detail to make sure a brand campaign reaches as many people as possible.

Outside of graphic design, Diego spends time honing in on digital illustrations for clients and other artists, collaborating in large groups and showcasing it on social media.
