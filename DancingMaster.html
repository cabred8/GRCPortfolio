Can I Call You a Dancin' Master?

Originally made in July of 2025, the piece was made for an event called ArtFight, an annual art gifting event. The piece features Beck Love, an original character who belongs to ArtFight user ANUKI. It uses aesthetics and graphical nods from Dance Dance Revolution Supernova 2. The piece was featured on display at the Donna Beam Art Gallery as part of the BA Call for Entry exhibit in February.
